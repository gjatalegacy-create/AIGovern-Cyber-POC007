# Public claims wording for the R48 draft

Editorial draft; no external publication has been performed. This wording contains no private absolute paths or unpublished implementation source. The internal source-and-hash ledger remains separate. Public capsule test results must be filled only from that exact released capsule.

**Origin and identity.** AIGovern-Cyber/POC007 is a bounded demonstration derived from the ESS-MAI research program of Bledar Gjata / Gjata Legacy. Its proposed constitutional method and terminology should retain that attribution. The verified [ORCID record](https://orcid.org/0009-0003-7854-266X) links the existing project identities. The earlier [v1.1.1 deposit](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22750188), dated 14 September 2026, contains POC003–006; its DOI must not be assigned to a new POC007 release.

| Hardware-adaptive control | Resource inputs and explicitly labeled estimates constrain workload permission under existing rules. | Numeric correctness and software refusal do not establish temperature accuracy, physical cooling or energy savings. |

**Hardware model and admission.** The wider implementation uses an inherited model relating CPU load and occupied memory to estimated power, then estimated temperature: `P_cpu=TDP×load/100`, `P_mem=k×usedGiB`, `T_est=T_ambient+R×(P_cpu+P_mem)`. A valid sensor may supply temperature where available; otherwise the estimate remains labeled as mathematical. The proposal concerns how resource availability constrains permitted computation. No new physical quantity or calibration is claimed. Unknown energy can still use an explicitly labeled estimate under current policy; there is no universal “unobserved energy always HOLD” guarantee. An actuator acknowledgment is recorded separately from physical effect, which remains unproven without independent readback.

**Reported internal evidence, separate from the open capsule.** The frozen R47 record reports 1,789 passing Cargo tests and four doctests, 82 native checks, 100 continuity checks and 57 fault checks. Hardware cases within that total include nine resource-domain, eleven thermal-domain and five actuation-contract cases, plus exact byte-budget and admission checks. These are internal observations in the retained environment, not independent replication or results of the public capsule. The confirmed adverse outcome must also be reported: replacing the store with a coherent older valid snapshot permits rollback. The scope therefore assumes trusted host/account controls and does not certify resistance to a hostile owner or administrator.

**Prior art and proposed contribution.** Existing failure-driven learning, access-control principles, proof-carrying interfaces, state-machine methods and runtime governance should be credited. The proposed contribution is the specific inspectable composition and its tested failure boundaries. Broad claims such as “first,” “universal,” “unbreakable” or “theory proved” are unsupported. The [Yad Vashem Besa collection](https://wwv.yadvashem.org/yv/en/exhibitions/besa/index.asp) supports cultural naming context; [Riesbeck's failure-driven learning](https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/81-1/Papers/024.pdf) and [Hammond's failure explanations](https://cdn.aaai.org/AAAI/1986/AAAI86-094.pdf) establish older failure-memory foundations.

The public POC should disclose its actual code/dependencies/license, exact command sequence, test inputs, adverse outcomes and claim limits. Retaining a larger private product must be stated explicitly; it cannot be described as independently reproduced by tests of a smaller capsule.
