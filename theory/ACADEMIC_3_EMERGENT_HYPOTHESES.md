# Academic 3 — Emergent Hypotheses

These are hypotheses suggested by the composition. They are not universal proofs.

## H-OPLS-1 — Dual-polarity closure obligation

A positive result cannot lawfully close if the exact request-bound negative counterpart was not searched and admitted for the same case/scope.

Falsifier: remove exact negative evidence while leaving positive evidence; if closure still succeeds, the hypothesis fails.

## H-OPLS-2 — Promise is process-law, not positive victory

BESA can remain fulfilled under a rigorous-negative outcome if the promise was to obey the dual-polarity processing law rather than force a constructive verdict.

Falsifier: a valid rigorous-negative trace cannot be distinguished from broken-promise failure.

## H-OPLS-3 — Fail-closed can be epistemically progressive

A fail-closed state can generate bounded gap evidence and a scoped next-i0 question without fabricating missing knowledge.

Falsifier: fail-closed produces only a terminal error or produces an ungrounded answer.

## H-OPLS-4 — Constructive success need not erase negative knowledge

A constructive result can retain relevant negative critique as an advancement/stress-test boundary in iZ.

Falsifier: constructive closure necessarily deletes or ignores all negative critique.

## H-OPLS-5 — Two-trace constitutional chain

A Light promise trace and an ASHT reasoning trace can carry different semantics while remaining cryptographically parent-bound and jointly consumable by NBES/Shadow.

Falsifier: either trace can be substituted across cases/scopes without rejection.
