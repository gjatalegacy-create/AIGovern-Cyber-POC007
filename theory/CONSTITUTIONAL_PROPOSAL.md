Vetëqeverisja Kushtetuese: një teori Governance-First për sistemet AI
Përmbledhje në shqip:
Një sistem AI nuk fiton të drejtën për të vepruar vetëm sepse ka aftësinë teknike për ta bërë atë veprim. E drejta për të vepruar lind vetëm kur përputhen tre gjëra: ligji i ekzekutueshëm, kompetenca e autoritetit dhe prova e kërkuar.
Në ESS-MAI, GCL (Gjata Collapse Law) është kushtetuta që përcakton kufijtë, por nuk merr vendimin në vend të autoriteteve. LIGHT koordinon, QUANTUM arsyeton, SHADOW verifikon dhe autorizon vendimin përfundimtar. Autoritetet nuk votojnë për një përgjigje të përbashkët; secili ushtron kompetencën e vet mbi provat që ka realisht para vetes.
Teoria dallon qartë dështimin e një autoriteti nga dështimi i ligjit. Kur dështon një autoritet, ligji mund të vazhdojë, dhe pasardhësi dëshmon mungesën e provës. Kur dështon vetë ligji, autoriteti hyn në HOLD dhe pret sqarim, pa e zëvendësuar ligjin me gjykimin e vet.
Çdo refuzim, pritje apo pranim duhet të ketë një bazë të dëshmueshme. Negative Knowledge ruan detyrimet e paplotësuara, ndërsa pozitivi fiton legjitimitet vetëm kur paraqet prova që i plotësojnë ato. Gjendja aktuale: rruga (0,0) është implementuar, ndërsa mbyllja e plotë e (1,1) është ende në zhvillim.
Ligji nuk zëvendëson gjykimin. Autoriteti nuk zëvendëson provën. Dështimi nuk e fshin përgjegjësinë.
Teksti i plotë në anglisht më poshtë.

CONSTITUTIONAL LAW–MONARCHY AUTHORITY SELF-GOVERNANCE
Deterministic • Verified • Low-Process
Constitutional Self-Governance as a Governance-First Theory for AI Systems
Bledar Gjata — GJATA, Governance Journey Advancing Traceable Al-bania
1. FOUNDATIONAL DEFINITION
Constitutional self-governance is a system’s capacity to instantiate governance within its own architecture: every authority operates within the jurisdiction conferred upon it by law, on the evidence that law requires, and must provide a verifiable account of both fulfillment and the inability to proceed.
A system does not acquire the right to alter the validity conditions of an action merely because it possesses the technical capability to perform that action. Nor may it substitute its own confidence, hierarchical position, or declaration of correctness for the evidence required.
Within this theory, “self” does not mean self-authorization. It means that governance is not external advice that a component may choose to follow or disregard. Governance is an intrinsic relationship among law, jurisdictional competence, evidence, and execution.
Accordingly:
“The system is governed by its own law, but it does not acquire unrestricted ownership of the law that governs it.”
This is the meaning of faithful and verifiable execution of the constitutional chain within ESS-MAI.
The theory does not prohibit learning, improvement, or the development of knowledge. It prohibits those processes from silently generating new jurisdictional powers, exemptions from the law, or self-granted acceptance criteria.
2. THE PROBLEM THE THEORY ADDRESSES
The fundamental problem is not merely whether an AI system can produce an incorrect answer. It is whether the system can confer more authority upon an answer, conclusion, or action than its supporting basis warrants.
One component may generate persuasive language. Another may perform a correct calculation. A third may occupy a senior decision-making position. None of these properties, considered independently, establishes that the resulting action is constitutionally valid within the system.
The theory distinguishes four concepts:
Capability: what the component is technically able to do.
Jurisdictional competence: what the component is authorized to do in the particular case.
Evidence: the inspectable support available for the proposed action.
Judgment: the conclusion that the evidence warrants within the authority’s assigned role.
These concepts must not substitute for one another.
Capability does not create jurisdiction. Jurisdiction does not create evidence. Recorded evidence is not automatically an adjudicated conclusion. Hierarchical seniority does not validate an unsupported judgment.
Governance-first means establishing these relationships before a consequential action occurs, rather than retrospectively after execution. Monitoring and post-execution analysis may remain part of the system, but they do not replace the preconditions for authorization.
3. GCL: A CONSTITUTION THAT CONSTRAINS WITHOUT USURPING
In this formulation, GCL — Gjata Collapse Law provides the constitutional foundation that determines the permissible relationships among authorities.
GCL specifies jurisdictions, activation conditions, evidentiary obligations, transition boundaries, and failure-handling requirements. It must not, however, become a concealed decision-maker that deprives the authorities of their assigned functions.
Two levels must be distinguished.
The GCL constitution establishes the overall order: which authority may perform which operation, under what conditions, and with what responsibilities.
The law executed within a particular flow is the specific obligation or law-governed process that the competent authorities must carry out.
This distinction is essential when stating that “the law failed.” A particular law or its execution path may halt and require clarification while the constitutional order continues to determine who may review the failure and how the authorities awaiting that law must respond.
Consequently, the suspension of a law does not release components from constitutional constraints. The constitution itself governs their waiting state and the limits of their non-intervention.
Likewise, the fact that the law continues does not establish that the preceding authority fulfilled its obligation. Continuity of law and fulfillment of an obligation are distinct properties.
4. CONSTITUTIONAL MONARCHY AND THE TWO INVARIANTS
Here, “monarchy” denotes the relationship between the final adjudicative authority and the constitution that bounds the validity of its authority. It is not an argument for a political regime in human society.
SHADOW, through the relevant GJ-LGC authority, exercises judgment within the jurisdiction conferred by GCL. It is sovereign within its assigned competence, but not sovereign over the conditions that make that competence valid.
First invariant: the sovereign cannot act outside the law
An authority may not unilaterally expand its jurisdiction, declare mandatory evidence unnecessary, or authorize an action merely because it considers the outcome beneficial.
Even a desirable result remains inadmissible when the path that produced it exceeded the applicable mandate.
Second invariant: the law cannot decide in place of the sovereign
The law determines the boundaries and conditions of judgment. It must not appropriate the function assigned to the adjudicative authority.
Prohibiting an action outside a jurisdiction is not equivalent to making every decision within that jurisdiction.
This does not require multiple admissible answers for every input. It requires that the adjudicative function be exercised by the designated authority, rather than displaced by another component that predetermines the outcome and requests only formal ratification.
Together, these invariants express mutual non-corruption:
“Authority must not appropriate the law. The law must not divest authority of its assigned competence.”
An authority’s independence is not independence from evidence. It is independence to exercise its own competence over that evidence without being compelled to reproduce its predecessor’s conclusion.
5. DISTINCT AUTHORITIES, NOT VOTING TOWARD A COMMON ANSWER
Within ESS-MAI, the fundamental separation remains:
LIGHT coordinates. QUANTUM reasons. SHADOW verifies and authorizes the final decision.
BESA, ASHT, and NBES operate within these jurisdictions. They are not three coequal sovereigns negotiating an outcome. The implementation-governance document preserves this separation, placing ASHT under QUANTUM’s orchestration and final verification under SHADOW.
BESA does not discharge the reasoning obligation merely by organizing the promise. ASHT does not discharge NBES’s adjudicative obligation merely by completing its reasoning. NBES must not declare acceptance solely because its predecessor reports success.
Evidence communication is necessary; prior agreement on the verdict is not the mechanism of legitimation.
This is why provenance preservation must not become an obligation to harmonize interpretations.
The trace must retain the identity of what was requested, what was processed, and what was produced. At the same time, the successor authority must retain the competence to determine that the material presented is insufficient.
If NBES determines that the evidence does not establish fulfillment of BESA’s promise, it has no obligation to revise its conclusion to align with ASHT. Its obligation is to handle the discrepancy under the applicable law and provide an evidentiary account of it.
A constitutional outcome is not the product of consensus. It is the product of duly exercised jurisdictional competences.
6. THE CENTRAL DISTINCTION: FAILURE OF AUTHORITY VERSUS FAILURE OF LAW
This distinction is one of the theory’s defining features.
When an authority fails, the law may continue
ASHT may fail to fulfill its reasoning obligation because exact candidates or the required evidence are unavailable.
That failure does not automatically halt the law. The law may continue to NBES.
NBES receives the same law but does not find the evidence that its predecessor was required to attach to the flow. NBES then also fails to achieve the required fulfillment, while attesting to the deficiency established within its own jurisdiction.
The law has continued; fulfillment of the obligation has not.
This is neither a conflict between laws nor a vote against ASHT. It is an independent determination that the evidentiary basis required for NBES to act was not presented.
When ASHT succeeds, NBES may still fail
Even if ASHT fulfills its own task, NBES’s fulfillment is not guaranteed.
Evidence may have been produced but may not be present, readable, bound to the case, or available for adjudication at the point where NBES must examine it.
NBES is not permitted to reason:
“My predecessor states that the evidence exists; therefore, I will behave as though I have examined it.”
It must attest to the state actually available to it.
Evidence that a record was produced does not automatically establish that the record reached the successor authority and was verified there.
When the law itself fails, the authority enters HOLD
In this case, the authority does not receive the executable law for which it holds jurisdictional competence.
It does not replace the law, complete it according to its own judgment, or initiate an adjudicative process that the missing law was supposed to authorize.
Two distinct accounts are required: the law’s execution path must attest to the cause of its suspension, while the waiting authority must attest that it did not receive the law necessary to exercise its assigned competence.
The authority remains in HOLD pending review and clarification by the competent jurisdiction.
Situation → Status of the law → Required authority behavior:
▪ The predecessor does not produce the required evidence. → The law continues. → The successor establishes the deficiency and fails with an evidentiary account.
▪ The predecessor fulfills its task, but the evidence is unavailable to the successor. → The law continues. → The successor does not inherit success; it attests to its own evidentiary boundary.
▪ The law continues beyond an authority’s jurisdiction. → The law remains valid. → The authority does not expand its mandate; it stops and records the reason.
▪ The law halts and requires clarification. → The law does not arrive for the relevant execution. → The authority enters HOLD and awaits authorized review.
▪ The law and required evidence arrive. → Execution may be considered. → The authority exercises its competence; a positive outcome is not predetermined.
The resulting principle is:
“Closure of the success path does not necessarily entail closure of the law’s path or the evidentiary path.”
Moreover, “authority failure” does not necessarily indicate a software defect. It may constitute correct enforcement of the obligation not to proceed without evidence. A component that refuses lawfully may have performed its control function correctly even though the obligation to achieve positive fulfillment remains unmet.
7. WITNESSING: THE SYSTEM MUST ACCOUNT FOR ITS OWN INABILITY TO PROCEED
Within this theory, failure is not an information-free output.
An error message, a refusal label, or a 0 is insufficient. The relationship among the expected condition, the available state, the attempted action, and the reason for stopping must remain inspectable.
D-BAB witnesses the pre-processing condition and the post-processing outcome. LGC preserves the trace. Neither replaces judgment.
Recording a claim does not make that claim true. Witnessing an event does not confer sovereign competence upon the witness.
A useful evidentiary account must identify which authority was responsible, what jurisdiction it held, which condition it was required to satisfy, and what it actually established.
The same requirement applies to absence.
If NBES does not find evidence in its input, it may attest:
“The required evidence was not available at this point.”
It may not automatically conclude:
“No one ever produced that evidence.”
Similarly, an authority that did not receive the law may attest to non-arrival. The cause of the law’s failure must be supported by evidence from the path where it stopped, rather than inferred without support by the waiting authority.
Witnessed absence is information about a specified condition at a specified point. It is not unrestricted knowledge about everything that was not observed.
If the same deficiency causes ASHT to fail and subsequently causes NBES to fail, two observations exist with distinct roles. They do not thereby become two causally independent pieces of evidence. The trace must preserve the relationship between them.
8. NEGATIVE, FAIL-CLOSED, AND HOLD ARE NOT SYNONYMS
Three categories must remain distinct.
NEGATIVE is an epistemic category: what is learned from an objection, an unmet obligation, or a witnessed failure processed under the applicable law.
FAIL-CLOSED is an execution constraint: the path does not acquire the right to proceed with the unauthorized action.
HOLD is an operational waiting state: the authority does not exercise its competence for the requested action until the conditions for authorized handling are satisfied.
A failure may produce negative evidence while simultaneously closing an execution path. This does not make Negative Knowledge identical to closure.
An authority may remain in HOLD without having issued a final negative judgment on the substantive content of the request.
Nor should every absence or anomaly be classified as HOLD. The implementation-governance document distinguishes digest, signature, or seal integrity failures from operational deficiencies that may be handled through a waiting state. An integrity violation must not be disguised as ordinary ambiguity awaiting clarification.
This distinction protects the theory against a fundamental error: describing as “self-governance” a system that merely collapses every problem into the same refusal.
9. NEGATIVE KNOWLEDGE AS ACCOUNTABILITY FOR SUCCESS
In this formulation, the negative is not merely an adversary of the positive. It bears responsibility for identifying what must be fulfilled before the positive can acquire legitimacy.
The positive must do more than present supporting material. It must demonstrate that the support withstands the relevant objections and satisfies the obligations of the case.
A proposition may be true and nevertheless provide insufficient evidence for the conclusion being sought.
In the flag example, the issue is not to deny the colors. It is whether the submitted description satisfies the requirement for exact identification. A general truth does not automatically authorize a specific identification.
The governing question therefore shifts from:
“Is there anything positive in this answer?”
to:
“Does this answer satisfy the precise burden of proof for this conclusion?”
Within the theory, the negative is examined first, and the positive must surpass it under the applicable evidentiary rules. The negative is retained as a basis of accountability, not discarded as soon as a positive outcome becomes available.
Objection itself must also remain accountable. It must identify a deficiency, inconsistency, or counterexample within the subject matter of the judgment. It does not acquire the right to invent an unbounded sequence of requirements outside its jurisdiction merely to keep the positive blocked.
Vector aggregation and weighting
For OPL2S, the specified aggregation method is the arithmetic mean of the positive vectors and, separately, the arithmetic mean of the negative vectors:
P̄ = (P₁ + P₂ + … + Pₙ₊) / n₊
N̄ = (N₁ + N₂ + … + Nₙ₋) / n₋
QUANTUM performs the corresponding computation. SHADOW exercises judgment over the basis presented to it, under the applicable law.
Aggregation is not itself the verdict. The averaging operation determines each side’s final vector. The comparison rule, the interpretation of the dimensions, and candidate admissibility must be specified in the relevant contract.
When exact candidates are missing, computation cannot fabricate them. When a candidate set is empty, its mean cannot automatically be treated as a valid zero vector.
A high positive weight also does not replace evidence that the authority received the law and the records required for adjudication.
Two distinct questions are therefore preserved: the strength of the evidence and the entitlement to act upon that evidence.
10. FROM OBJECTION TO INQUIRY: ADVANCING THE USER’S UNDERSTANDING
Self-governance does not terminate at “no.”
Within the OPL2S flow, a witnessed deficiency may become the basis of a question returned to the user within the prescribed scope (scope fix).
0-NOI signals the need to address the deficiency. It does not create the missing fact, issue a verdict, or appropriate adjudicative power. The structured question is instantiated by the relevant flow, binding the request for clarification to the evidence and to iZ.
Within the described flow, KiE may provide initial candidates and contribute to the conclusion of the processed branch. Initial candidates, however, do not automatically constitute sufficient evidence for ASHT or NBES.
The system therefore communicates something more precise than “try again”:
“For this conclusion, at this point in processing, this evidence is missing. This is the question that requires clarification.”
The user’s response enters the system as new material for examination. It does not become true or sufficient merely because the user submitted it.
This is the intended progression: the user refines the formulation and its evidentiary basis; the system reevaluates it under the applicable rules without lowering the standard or rewriting the law.
iZ is not merely a closed result. In this relationship, it carries the meaning of what was or was not fulfilled and the direction of the next clarification.
This does not imply that every HOLD is iZ or that every iZ represents positive acceptance. Operational state, epistemic outcome, and the continuation of inquiry must retain their distinct meanings.
11. DETERMINISTIC, VERIFIED, AND LOW-PROCESS
These three properties impose different requirements. None automatically establishes the other two.
Deterministic: the same complete case, the same process
Determinism concerns more than the wording of a question. It concerns the complete decision-relevant state: the law, evidence, relevant history, candidates, parameters, and event ordering.
An analytical representation is:
F(C, s, i, e) = (s′, r, τ)
Here, C represents the constitution and applicable rules, s the state, i the input, e the evidence, r the result, and τ the trace.
The requirement is that identical complete conditions produce the same outcome and the same semantic processing path. Technical identifiers that vary between executions must be distinguished from substantive changes in the decision.
When the user introduces new evidence, the case is no longer identical. A different outcome may therefore be the correct behavior rather than a violation of determinism.
An authority’s freedom is jurisdictional competence, not randomness.
Verified: an inspectable relationship, not self-attestation
“Verified” requires an inspectable relationship among law, authority, evidence, action, and outcome.
A trace supports reconstruction of what occurred. A test examines a particular behavior. A formal proof has its own subject matter and assumptions. These must not be represented as equivalent levels of verification.
A seal or hash may bind the identity of a record, but it does not replace judgment concerning the record’s substantive content.
Verification is a demonstrable relationship, not merely a “PASS” label attached to an outcome.
Low-Process: computational economy without diminished accountability
Low-Process does not mean removing controls that obstruct an easy success.
It means avoiding unnecessary replication of the constitutional mechanism within every component, constraining side effects, and concentrating consequential transitions at specified boundaries.
Computation may be designed to remain pure within its assigned function, while writing, persistence, or actions upon resources are treated as distinct effects requiring authorization.
Zero-copy transport and non-duplication of authority are separate objectives. The first concerns data movement; the second concerns the right to act. Neither automatically establishes the other.
Actual cost must be measured in execution time, memory consumption, and computational work. A small byte footprint is a separate property and does not, by itself, establish Low-Process.
12. IMPLICIT LAW: NOT SECRET, BUT NOT REDECLARED WITHIN EVERY COMPONENT
“Implicit law” does not mean unknown or unspecified law.
It means that each component must not maintain a constitution of its own and independently decide how to apply it to the transition it seeks.
GCL takes effect at jurisdictional boundaries and the corresponding transitions, including controlled interfaces and capabilities such as FFI and CapHandle.
The component performs its assigned function; it does not choose whether constitutional enforcement applies.
This principle nevertheless carries a concrete proof obligation: every path that produces an authoritative effect must traverse the relevant boundary. A side path that bypasses that control would contradict the architectural claim.
Accordingly:
“The law need not be declared within every component, but it must not be absent from the transition that gives an action operative authority.”
This also explains why the architecture should not require an additional supervisor with unrestricted power to bypass the system. Every reviewing authority remains bounded by its own mandate.
13. LOGICAL FORMULATION OF THE RELATIONSHIP
The following notation is an analytical expression of the principles described above, not a new invariant that replaces the canonical contracts.
Let a denote an authority, ℓ the applicable law, e the evidence, and x the requested action.
A permitted authoritative effect must imply:
AllowedEffect(a, ℓ, e, x) ⇒ LawReady(ℓ) ∧ Competent(a, ℓ, x) ∧ EvidenceSufficient(e, ℓ, x)
This states a necessary condition, not an algorithm that appropriates the authority’s judgment. Satisfying the input conditions establishes the basis for exercising competence; it does not predetermine every conclusion.
Evidentiary requirements are action-dependent. Evidence sufficient to attest to an absence is not the same as evidence sufficient to authorize positive success.
Where the law arrives but the required evidence does not, the intended consequence is that the action remains unauthorized, the deficiency is witnessed, and continuation is handled under the applicable law.
Where failure of the law prevents its arrival, the waiting authority does not execute the task. It attests to its waiting state and enters HOLD pending authorized review.
Loss of entitlement to perform the requested action does not extinguish the obligation to attest to why that action was not permitted. These are distinct competences.
14. SELF-GOVERNANCE ADAPTS TO THE SYSTEM, NOT TO THE DESIRED OUTCOME
Self-governance need not be instantiated through identical module names, an identical system scale, or the same execution flow in every product.
It adapts to the function being governed, the type of evidence involved, the consequences of the action, and the corresponding allocation of jurisdictional competences.
What must be preserved is the constitutional relationship: law constrains, authority exercises its competence, evidence provides support, and failure is witnessed.
OPL2S / POC007 instantiates this relationship at its own scale and is intended as an independent product. Its subject matter is obligation, BESA as a promise, and evidenced fulfillment.
PD / Digital Lab / Spine9 constitutes a different flow and a separate product. There, self-governance applies to laboratory activity, hypothesis development, and the evidentiary requirements for evaluating a project.
Neither must become a module of the other for both to share the same constitutional foundation.
Canonical source extraction aims to preserve the semantics and responsibilities of the extracted flow. Reintegration must preserve those relationships, not merely assemble files that compile successfully together.
Adaptability changes the scope of application; it does not negotiate away the invariant.
15. WHAT MUST BE DEMONSTRATED, AND WHAT THE TERMINOLOGY DOES NOT GUARANTEE
The theory becomes testable when every claim is accompanied by a condition that could refute it.
An action producing an effect without mandatory evidence would contradict the authorization requirement. NBES accepting solely on the basis of ASHT’s reported success would contradict independent judgment. An authority proceeding with its task when the applicable law has not arrived would contradict the HOLD boundary.
Likewise, a system that rejects a fully valid case because of requirements invented outside the law would expose a different problem: not insufficient rigor, but an inability to recognize fulfillment.
This is why (0,0) and (1,1) matter together.
Within this model, they represent the respective complete states of refusal and acceptance. They do not imply that every internal state is binary or that HOLD must be forced into a verdict.
Uncertainty is permitted as an explicitly declared state; it is not permitted to masquerade as completeness.
Compliance with a law does not automatically establish that the law adequately addresses every possible risk. Any guarantee must specify its subject matter, assumptions, boundaries, and conditions of applicability.
Accordingly, “guaranteed success” must mean success that, when authorized, satisfies the specified and verified conditions, not a promise that every request will inevitably produce a positive outcome.
At the reported stage of development, the (0,0) path has been implemented, while complete closure of (1,1) remains under development. This distinguishes the formulation of the theory from the scope of the evidence currently reported for its implementation.
16. RESEARCH QUESTIONS AND CONSOLIDATED FORMULATION
The central research question is:
“Can a system govern itself through an algorithmic constitution that constrains every authority without appropriating its competence, preserves continuity of law beyond the failure of an individual authority, and suspends execution when the law itself requires clarification, while making every such transition evidence-bearing and reproducible?”
For the relationship with Negative Knowledge and OPL2S:
“Can an evidenced objection become responsible for advancing success by identifying the obligations that the positive must fulfill, without acquiring the power to reject valid fulfillment arbitrarily?”
The proposed ESS-MAI contribution lies in organizing these relationships into an executable architecture, not merely in the existence of tracing, refusal, witnessing, or role separation. The originality claim concerns the roles, their composition, and their boundaries, to be examined through implementation and falsifiable counterexamples.
Consolidated statement of the theory
Constitutional Law–Monarchy Authority Self-Governance proposes a computational order in which entitlement to act derives not from a component’s power, but from the alignment of executable law, jurisdictional competence, and evidence.
Authorities exercise distinct functions without replacing adjudication with consensus. Failure of an authority does not automatically invalidate the law; failure of the law does not authorize a component to replace it. Every fulfillment, refusal, or waiting state must possess a demonstrable basis.
Negative Knowledge preserves the record of unmet obligations, while the positive acquires legitimacy only by presenting evidence that fulfills them.
Self-governance is therefore not freedom to invent the rules during execution. It is the capacity to act, halt, and resume only within the constitutional order that makes those operations valid.
Law does not replace judgment. Authority does not replace evidence. Failure does not erase accountability; it makes it subject to scrutiny.
