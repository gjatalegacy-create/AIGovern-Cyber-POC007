# Academic 2 — ESS-MAI Method Composition

ESS-MAI does not require `one formula = one phase`.

A formula may appear more than once under GCL if its **method binding** changes:

```text
MethodBinding = Formula + Location + Purpose + Inputs + Outputs + AuthorityBoundary + FailureSemantics
```

POC 007 uses that principle:

- 0-NOI's documented zero-authority vector is used only as a trigger.
- Obligation logic is used only to make dual-polarity closure mandatory.
- Collision logic binds the positive and negative to the same case/scope.
- DBAB witnesses/binds the law instance; it does not reason.
- BESA promises the exact law instance.
- ASHT performs exact-pair admission/reasoning trace.
- NBES checks fulfilment of the promise.
- Shadow verifies the chain.
- iZ carries the next advancement question.

The method is therefore a puzzle/composition of bounded formulas under GCL rather than a single all-purpose equation.
